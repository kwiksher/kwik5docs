Details by Feature

Images with Text Rendering

  • Gemini 2.0 and OpenAI (DALL·E/Sora) both excel at rendering readable, accurate text within images[4].
  • Grok can render text but struggles with fidelity and may produce odd or less polished results[4].

Multi-Scene Image Generation

  • Gemini 2.0 supports multi-turn, memory-based edits, enabling stepwise scene changes[4].
  • Sora, as a video model, is designed for multi-scene generation and likely supports this for static images.
  • Grok can generate images with multiple concepts, but lacks fine control over transitions and memory for sequential edits[4].

Image Editing

  • Both Gemini 2.0 and Grok allow for object insertion/removal, background swaps, and style changes, but Gemini maintains higher consistency and fidelity[4][5][6].
  • Grok is more creative but less predictable-edits may unintentionally change other image aspects[5][6].
  • Face and character edits are more stable in Gemini; Grok may alter pose or facial features unintentionally[5].
  • Picture-in-picture and compositional edits are supported by all, but Gemini offers more precise control[4].

OpenAI Sora

  • Sora is primarily a video generation model, but OpenAI’s image models (like DALL·E 3 and ChatGPT-4o) are leading in text rendering, multi-scene, and advanced editing[4].
  • Sora’s capabilities for static image editing are inferred from OpenAI’s broader ecosystem, as direct image editing features are not the primary focus of Sora.

Summary

  • Gemini 2.0: Best for precise, consistent, and high-fidelity image editing, especially when multiple sequential edits or context preservation are needed[4][5][6].
  • OpenAI Sora: Excels at multi-scene and text rendering; static image editing strengths align with DALL·E and ChatGPT-4o, which are industry leaders in these tasks[4].
  • Grok: Strong in creative, less restrictive edits, but less reliable for professional or highly controlled image editing; may introduce unexpected changes[4][5][6].

If you need professional, reliable edits and text in images, Gemini 2.0 and OpenAI’s tools are superior. For rapid, creative, and social media-style edits, Grok is a strong choice, albeit with less predictability and polish.
