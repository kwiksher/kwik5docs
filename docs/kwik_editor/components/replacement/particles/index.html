Particles

Sure! Here’s a brief overview of each emitter from the EmitterViewer directory:

  1. air_stars.json: Creates an effect with twinkling stars in the air.
  2. aurora_3b.json: Mimics the beautiful aurora borealis with colorful light waves.
  3. big_orange_flame.json: Produces a large, intense orange flame effect.
  4. blood.json: Simulates blood splatter.
  5. blue_galaxy.json: Forms a swirling blue galaxy.
  6. blue_vortex_field.json: Generates a vortex field with a blue hue.
  7. bp_firefly_final.json: Represents fireflies glowing intermittently.
  8. comet.json: Shows a comet streaking across the sky.
  9. crazy_blue.json: Creates a chaotic, vibrant blue pattern.
  10. electrons.json: Displays electrons moving in a dynamic, electrifying pattern.
  11. fireplace_flame.json: Produces a cozy fireplace flame effect.
  12. giving.json: Themed particles that give a sense of spreading or giving out.
  13. heart04.json: Forms hearts in various patterns.
  14. hongshizi.json: Displays red cross particles.
  15. im_seeing_stars.json: Generates a dizzying effect of stars.
  16. lava_flow.json: Simulates flowing lava.
  17. my_galaxy.json: Creates a personalized galaxy effect.
  18. real_popcorn.json: Simulates popping popcorn.
  19. smoke.json: Produces smoke rising.
  20. trippy.json: Creates a psychedelic, trippy visual effect.
  21. water_fountain.json: Forms a water fountain with particles mimicking splashes.
  22. waterfall.json: Displays a cascading waterfall effect.
  23. wdemitter.json: A generic emitter for various effects.

Feel free to explore these effects and see which one fits your project best! Let me know if you need more detailed information about any specific emitter.
